# Factual Claims from Dissertation

## Simulation Stability (Phase 1)
- **Claim 1.1:** The LS-DYNA simulations achieved high thermodynamic stability with a maximum energy drift of -0.0329% (observed in the Bamboo-HDPE model).
- **Claim 1.2:** Hourglass energy remained at 0.00 J throughout all simulations, confirming the validity of the element formulations and mesh.

## Force Transmission (Phase 2 - EN 397)
- **Claim 2.1:** All candidate materials (Virgin HDPE, ABS, Bamboo-HDPE, Recycled PC) passed the EN 397 safety criterion of < 5.0 kN transmitted force.
- **Claim 2.2:** Filtered peak force for Virgin HDPE was 4.05 kN.
- **Claim 2.3:** Filtered peak force for ABS Plastic was 2.25 kN.
- **Claim 2.4:** Filtered peak force for Bamboo-HDPE 30% wt was 4.51 kN.
- **Claim 2.5:** Filtered peak force for Recycled PC (rPC) was 4.54 kN.

## Benchmarking & Validation
- **Claim 3.1:** The Virgin HDPE baseline (4.05 kN) is consistent with experimental ranges (3.0–4.5 kN) reported by Tham et al. (2014) and simulation ranges (3.2–3.8 kN) by Long et al. (2015).

## Energy Absorption (Phase 3)
- **Claim 4.1:** Virgin HDPE recorded the highest internal strain energy absorption (27.35 J, 55.3% of impact energy).
- **Claim 4.2:** ABS Plastic absorbed 26.21 J (52.8%) as internal energy.
- **Claim 4.3:** Bamboo-HDPE 30% wt absorbed 22.84 J (46.1%) as internal energy.
- **Claim 4.4:** Recycled PC absorbed the least internal energy (16.67 J, 33.5%), acting primarily as an elastic spring.

## Structural Displacement (Phase 4)
- **Claim 5.1:** Virgin HDPE exhibited the highest structural crush depth at 18.42 mm.
- **Claim 5.2:** Recycled PC exhibited the lowest structural crush depth at 10.23 mm.

## Oblique Impact & Rotational Kinematics (Phase 5)
- **Claim 6.1:** Recycled PC demonstrated a 74% reduction in peak rotational acceleration (896 rad/s²) compared to Virgin HDPE (3482 rad/s²).
- **Claim 6.2:** Bamboo-HDPE 30% wt recorded a significantly higher peak rotational acceleration of 12044 rad/s².
- **Claim 6.3:** Material modulus is a primary factor in rotational acceleration generation; higher flexural rigidity (rPC) promotes lower tangential frictional interaction.

## Mechanical Mechanisms
- **Claim 7.1:** Ductile materials (HDPE) neutralize impact energy primarily through internal strain energy yielding, extending impact duration but increasing crush depth.
- **Claim 7.2:** High-modulus materials (ABS, rPC) protect via load distribution due to increased flexural stiffness, distributing forces across a broader area of the EPS liner.
